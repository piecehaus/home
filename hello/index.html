Dear my future roommates,



Hello! My name is Jung, and I’m a video-maker and essayist from South Korea.
I'll be studying at Minerva Art Academy from September until the end of January, so I’m looking for a room to stay during my exchange.

A little about me : I love watching animation, reading non-fiction books, cooking in the kitchen, and sharing thoughts with people from different cultures. At home, I value a calm and comfortable environment. In my free time, I enjoy going for walks, journaling and making fun things like this page.

I spent three months studying in Iceland, where I lived in a shared house with fifteen students from different countries. Sharing cooking and cleaning responsibilities taught me how important communication and consideration are when living together.

After that, I travelled around Europe through CouchSurfing and WWOOF, and these experiences broadened my life perspective and inspired me to continue learning from people with different cultural backgrounds. When I stayed in Berlin for three weeks, I shared a flat with three friends. During the day we each had own routines, but in the evenings we played pingpong, watched movies, and ate dinner together. It was a great experience to get to know what makes a good shared living. These experiences taught me what makes a good shared living, and that's what I'm hoping to find again in Groningen.


Ideally, I’m looking for a place where registration is possible, and my budget is around €400-€600 per month. I’d be grateful for the opportunity to meet via video call, as I’m currently in South Korea until July.

I’m hoping to find a place that feels like a community for the next few months.
Thank you for reading, and I hope to hear from you!

Jung
